Privacy Policy

Effective date: [insert date you publish this] · Applies to [your business/legal entity name] ("JarCheck," "we," "us")

This policy explains what information JarCheck collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

  • Account & subscription info: your email address, and subscription/trial status.
  • Recipe content: the recipe text, titles, and jar-size selections you submit to the Safety Shield Analyzer, so we can return an analysis to you.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe shares back only what we need to manage your subscription (e.g., subscription status, a customer ID).
  • Photos: if you submit a photo to the Community Hub, it's briefly analyzed for content-moderation purposes.
  • Basic usage data: standard technical data (like browser type) that's collected automatically by any website.
  • Local browser storage: we store a small flag in your browser to remember that your trial is active, so you don't have to re-verify on every visit.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the Safety Shield Analyzer, digital pantry, jar labels, and weekly recipe features.
  • To create and manage your subscription and trial period.
  • To send you the welcome email and, if you opt in, the weekly recipe email. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the site.

Service Providers We Use

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers solely to operate JarCheck. They only receive the information necessary to perform their function, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Supabase — secure database storage for subscriber and account records.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and newsletter emails.
  • Google (Gemini API) — powers the AI recipe-safety analysis, photo moderation, chat assistant, and AI-drafted email content. Recipe text and photos you submit are sent to this API to generate your results.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

Data Retention

We keep subscriber records for as long as your account/subscription is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, tax, and record-keeping purposes. You can request deletion of your data at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

Your Rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails anytime via the link in any email we send.
  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us at [support email].
  • Cancel your subscription anytime via the "Manage Subscription" link in the site footer, or directly through Stripe.

Children's Privacy

JarCheck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard providers (Stripe, Supabase) that maintain strong security practices to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.
